LITTLE DUDE AND HIS WEIRD BROTHERS / MELD CLAY v1

Requires Meld Clay v1 and Blender 4.5 or newer.
Saved in Blender 4.5.0; reopened and rendered in Blender 5.1.2.
Guide: https://theverycob.github.io/meld-clay-guide/#first-model
Offline: ../../index.html#first-model

Save a working copy. Select the clay body and click Edit Parts to explore its
blockout. Finish Editing Parts hides the cages. Eyes remain separate, attached
clay eyes. Use Move Whole Model to move the character together with its parts.

Little-Dude-Quirky.blend: the original six parts, mint Soft Dough, Form variation
12 and Random seed 1. The guide compares seeds 1, 4 and 8 before adding eyes.
Brother-Noodle.blend: curled source arms, Thumb pressed shaping, a colored Cat
iris and Glazed Ceramic. A Pressed join marks the curled shoulder.
Brother-Puff.blend: overlapping lobes, three feet, Gently kneaded shaping,
Compression and Heart / Star / Round pupils.
Brother-Crumb.blend: a beveled box body, Hold Shape, Terracotta, Dry Fractures,
small bite impressions and Diamond / Cross pupils.
Little-Dude-Family.blend: all four live models in one ivory studio.

These character names are example recipes, not new dropdown presets.
Each individual file contains a START HERE text with its settings and source
construction. The base shape is part of the recipe: a seed cannot add arms or
eyes. Begin with the supplied example to reproduce the illustrated character.

F12 renders the included camera and lights. Studio objects are hidden only in
the viewport. Cycles is saved on CPU; select your own GPU for faster rendering.
No external textures or linked libraries are required.
Original example artwork: GPL-3.0-or-later, as supplied with Meld Clay.
